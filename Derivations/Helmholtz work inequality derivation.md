**Setup:** Isolated system at constant $T$ (in contact with heat bath) and constant $V$. System starts and ends at same $V, T$. Only interaction with surroundings is heat exchange.

Energy conservation:
$$W + \Delta E_\text{sys} - Q = 0 \implies W = Q - \Delta E_\text{sys}$$

Entropy change of bath: $\Delta S_\text{bath} = -Q/T$

By the 2nd law ($\Delta S_	ext{total} \geq 0$):
$$\Delta S_\text{sys} \geq \frac{Q}{T}$$
equality iff reversible.

Free energy change:
$$\Delta F_\text{sys} = \Delta E_\text{sys} - T\,\Delta S_\text{sys} \leq \Delta E_\text{sys} - T\cdot\frac{Q}{T} = \Delta E_\text{sys} - Q = -W$$

**Result:**
$$\boxed{W \leq -\Delta F}$$
equality iff reversible.

**Corollary ($W=0$):** $\Delta F \leq 0$ — at constant $T, V$ with no work, free energy never increases.
