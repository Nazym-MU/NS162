**Starting point:** On the coexistence curve, phases 1 and 2 have equal Gibbs free energy at $(P, T)$:
$$E_1 + PV_1 - TS_1 = E_2 + PV_2 - TS_2 	ag{1}$$

At the nearby point $(P + dP,\, T + dT)$, also on the curve:
$$E_1 + (P+dP)V_1 - (T+dT)S_1 = E_2 + (P+dP)V_2 - (T+dT)S_2 	ag{2}$$

Subtract (1) from (2):
$$dP\cdot V_1 - dT\cdot S_1 = dP\cdot V_2 - dT\cdot S_2$$

Rearrange:
$$\boxed{\frac{dP}{dT} = \frac{S_1 - S_2}{V_1 - V_2}} 	ag{3}$$

**Latent heat form:** At constant $T$ on the coexistence curve, $dE = T\,dS$, so integrating:
$$E_2 - E_1 = T(S_2 - S_1)$$

Substituting into (3):
$$\boxed{\frac{dP}{dT} = \frac{E_2 - E_1}{T(V_1 - V_2)}} 	ag{4}$$

Slope of the coexistence curve is proportional to the latent heat $E_2 - E_1$.
