Liouville's Theorem: The volume of any closed surface in phase space remains constant as that surface moves through phase space. This is a consequence of trajectories never crossing.
$$\Rightarrow \nabla \cdot v = \nabla \cdot \dot z = 0$$
Apply divergence theorem to Hamilton's equations:
$$\dot z = \left( \frac{\partial H}{\partial p}, - \frac{\partial H}{\partial q} \right)$$
$$\nabla \cdot \dot z = \frac{\partial}{\partial q} \frac{\partial H}{\partial p} + \frac{\partial}{\partial p} \left( -\frac{\partial H}{\partial q} \right) = 0$$
