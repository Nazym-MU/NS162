Terminology:
- Statistical Mechanics - study of how the phenomena of the macroscopic world emerge from the microscopic world
- Classical Thermodynamics (and the laws of thermodynamics)
- Entropy - measures a system's disorder
- Equilibrium - a state of physical balance
- Macroscopic and microscopic properties - properties visible to a naked eye vs. behavior of individual atoms
- The Billiard Ball Model - the first modern atomic theory, viewing atoms as tiny, solid, indivisible spheres, much like billiard balls, with no internal structure
